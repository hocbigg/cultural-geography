# Summary

The Cultural Geography curriculum is a **complete education in Cultural Geography** using online materials.

Note: When there are courses or books that don't fit into the curriculum but are otherwise of high quality,
they belong in [extras/courses](extras/courses.md), [extras/readings](extras/readings.md) or [extras/other_curricula](extras/other_curricula.md).

**Process**. Students can work through the curriculum alone or in groups, in order or out of order.

- We recommend doing all courses in **Core**, only skipping a course when you are certain that you've already learned the material previously.
- For simplicity, we recommend working through courses (especially Core) in order from top to bottom. Some students choose to study multiple courses at a time in order to vary the material they are working on in a day/week.
- The courses in the Advanced section are electives. Choose one track to specialize in and complete all the courses listed under it.

# Curriculum

## Intro Cultural Geography

| Subject | Why Study? | Book | Online Course |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction to Human Geography | Provides core concepts of space, place, scale, and human-environment relations; essential for understanding cultural patterns spatially. | [*The Cultural Landscape: An Introduction to Human Geography*](https://archive.org/details/culturallandscap0012rube) (borrow on Internet Archive) | [Introduction to AP Human Geography](https://www.edx.org/learn/geography/the-university-of-texas-at-austin-introduction-to-ap-human-geography) (edX, free audit) |
| World Regional Geography | Offers overview of global cultural diversity, regions, and interconnections; contextualizes cultural processes. | [*World Regional Geography: People, Places and Globalization*](https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks/textbooks/world-regional-geography-people-places-and-globalization) (Open Textbook Library) | [World Regional Geography](https://learn.saylor.org/course/view.php?id=1145)  |

## Core Cultural Geography

| Subject | Why Study? | Book | Online Course |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Population Geography | Explores demographic patterns, growth, distribution, and health; foundation for understanding cultural diffusion and migration. | [*The Cultural Landscape: An Introduction to Human Geography*](https://archive.org/details/culturallandscap0012rube) (borrow on Internet Archive) | Covered in [Introduction to AP Human Geography](https://www.edx.org/learn/geography/the-university-of-texas-at-austin-introduction-to-ap-human-geography) |
| Migration and Mobility | Examines causes, patterns, and impacts of human movement; key to cultural mixing, diasporas, and globalization. | [*Human Geography: Places and Regions in Global Context*](https://www.amazon.com/Human-Geography-Places-Regions-Global/dp/032176966X) (Amazon link, no free found) | Covered in [Introduction to AP Human Geography](https://www.edx.org/learn/geography/the-university-of-texas-at-austin-introduction-to-ap-human-geography) |
| Cultural Geography Fundamentals | Core study of culture regions, landscapes, diffusion, ecology, and identity; defines the subfield. | [*The Human Mosaic*](https://archive.org/details/humanmosaicthema0000jord) (borrow on Internet Archive) | [Human Geography](https://und.edu/academics/online/enroll-anytime/geog151.html) (University of North Dakota self-paced) |
| Geography of Language and Religion | Analyzes spatial distribution and role in identity, conflict, and cultural persistence. | [*The Cultural Landscape: An Introduction to Human Geography*](https://archive.org/details/culturallandscap0012rube) | Covered in [Human Geography](https://und.edu/academics/online/enroll-anytime/geog151.html) |
| Political Geography | Investigates states, borders, geopolitics, and power; links culture to territory and nationalism. | [*Political Geography: World-Economy, Nation-State and Locality*](https://www.amazon.com/Political-Geography-World-economy-Nation-state-Locality/dp/0582357330) (Amazon link, no free found) | [Political Geography](https://www.mooc-list.com/course/political-geography-online-course) (MGIMO on edX/Coursera, free audit) |
| Economic Geography | Examines spatial patterns of production, consumption, and development; cultural influences on economies. | [*Economic Geography: A Contemporary Introduction*](https://www.amazon.com/Economic-Geography-A-Contemporary-Introduction/dp/1119389550) (Amazon link, no free found) | [Economic Geography of the Industrial World](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL8dPuuaLjXtO85Sl24rSiVQ93q7vcntNF) (UC Berkeley lectures on YouTube) |
| Urban Geography | Studies cities as cultural hubs, segregation, and globalization nodes. | [*Cities of the World*](https://www.amazon.com/Cities-World-World-Regional-Urban/dp/1538126346) (Amazon link, no free found) | Covered in [Human Geography](https://und.edu/academics/online/enroll-anytime/geog151.html) |

## Advanced Cultural Geography

### Culture, Identity, and Place

| Subject | Why Study? | Book | Online Course |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cultural Landscapes and Perception | Explores how humans imprint and interpret environments culturally. | [*The Human Mosaic*](https://archive.org/details/humanmosaicthema0000jord) | Covered in [Human Geography](https://und.edu/academics/online/enroll-anytime/geog151.html) |
| Ethnicity, Race, and Identity | Analyzes spatial aspects of social difference, inequality, and belonging. | [*Ethnicity and Race: Making Identities in a Changing World*](https://www.amazon.com/Ethnicity-Race-Making-Identities-Changing/dp/0761985018) (Amazon link) |  |
| Gender and Feminist Geography | Examines space through gender, power, and embodiment. | [*Feminist Geography Unbound*](https://www.amazon.com/Feminist-Geography-Unbound-Discounts-Differences/dp/1949199894) (Amazon link) |  |
| Postcolonial and Critical Cultural Geography | Critiques imperialism, representation, and power in cultural spaces. | [*Understanding Cultural Geography: Places and Traces*](https://www.amazon.com/Understanding-Cultural-Geography-Places-Traces/dp/1138815303) (Amazon link) |  |

### Globalization, Politics, and Development

| Subject | Why Study? | Book | Online Course |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Geographies of Globalization | Studies cultural homogenization/heterogenization and transnationalism. | [*Globalization and Diversity*](https://www.amazon.com/Globalization-Diversity-Geography-Environment-Exploration/dp/0134117018) (Amazon link) | Covered in [World Regional Geography](https://learn.saylor.org/course/view.php?id=1145) |
| Development Geography | Explores cultural dimensions of inequality, aid, and sustainability. | [*Geographies of Development*](https://www.amazon.com/Geographies-Development-Introduction-Studies/dp/1138794309) (Amazon link) |  |
| Geopolitics and Conflict | Analyzes cultural narratives in borders, territory, and war. | [*Political Geography: World-Economy, Nation-State and Locality*](https://www.amazon.com/Political-Geography-World-economy-Nation-state-Locality/dp/0582357330) | Covered in [Political Geography](https://www.mooc-list.com/course/political-geography-online-course) |
| Tourism and Mobility | Examines cultural exchange, commodification, and place-making. | [*The Wiley Blackwell Companion to Tourism*](https://www.amazon.com/Wiley-Blackwell-Companion-Tourism/dp/1118474481) (Amazon link) |  |

### Environment, Culture, and Society

| Subject | Why Study? | Book | Online Course |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Environmental Cultural Geography | Explores cultural views of nature, risks, and sustainability. | [*Placing Nature: Culture and Landscape Ecology*](https://archive.org/details/placingnaturecul0000unse_k4j6) (Amazon link) |  |

| Climate, Culture, and Society | Analyzes cultural responses to environmental change. |  | Covered in [Introduction to AP Human Geography](https://www.edx.org/learn/geography/the-university-of-texas-at-austin-introduction-to-ap-human-geography) |
